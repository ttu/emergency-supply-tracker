FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need an account?+

No. Open the app and start tracking immediately — there's no signup, no email, and nothing to verify.

Where is my data stored?+

Entirely on your own device, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server, so your household inventory stays completely private.

Does it really work offline?+

Yes. Once loaded, the app runs without an internet connection, so you can check and update your supplies during an outage.

Is it actually free?+

Completely. Every feature is included with no premium tiers or paywalls, and the code is open source.
